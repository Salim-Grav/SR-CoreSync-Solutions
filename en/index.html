Frequently Asked Questions

01

How long does custom software or ERP deployment typically take?

Typical mid-size platforms and ERP integrations take 4 to 8 weeks. We provide a milestone-driven roadmap and architecture blueprints prior to kickoff, accompanied by weekly live deployment demonstrations.

02

Do you provide services to international enterprises outside the Netherlands?

Absolutely. While our headquarters are in Amsterdam, Netherlands, we actively engineer solutions for enterprise clients across the European Union, the United Kingdom, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, and Kuwait through secure digital channels and executive reviews.

03

What distinguishes S&R CoreSync from conventional software agencies?

Our exclusive competitive advantage lies in the rare synergy between our two founding partners: a Principal Software & AI Architect (Partner S) and a Corporate Financial ERP Specialist (Partner R), ensuring that every technical solution serves profitability, business growth, and fiscal compliance from day one.

04

What are your contracting terms and milestone payment structures?

We operate under binding European service agreements compliant with Dutch law. Engagements are structured around transparent milestones, typically starting with an initial 50% mobilization retainer, with remaining funds due upon final user acceptance and verification.

05

Do you provide ongoing technical support, monitoring, and updates post-launch?

Yes. Every project includes an all-inclusive post-launch warranty period with proactive monitoring. We also provide flexible monthly CoreCare retainers to ensure continuous high-availability, zero-day security updates, and performance tuning.
